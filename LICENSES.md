# Copyright and Reuse

The repository is public for reading and citation, but public availability does not automatically grant permission to reproduce, modify, redistribute, or commercially exploit its contents.

Copyright and rights remain with the named author(s) and other applicable rights holders unless an individual work explicitly carries a separate license.

Each approved paper must identify its author(s), version, date, copyright/rights status, source attribution and any explicit reuse license before publication. Third-party figures, references, trademarks and quoted material retain their respective rights.

The public paper index should identify exact licenses when they have been approved. Do not infer an open-source license from the repository visibility setting.

For citation, follow the author, title, publication version, year and stable public identifier of the individual work.
