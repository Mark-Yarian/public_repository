## Source and current assessment

Introduces dendritic branching and cell diversity as motivations for richer computational units.

Human dendritic research supplies evidence for particular nonlinear cellular computations. It does not establish that copying tree-like geometry alone produces general intelligence or awareness.

## Research question

Which compartmental computations matter for a specified learning task?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Compare point-neuron and compartmental models with matched capacity and data; separate morphology, active properties, and learning rules.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
