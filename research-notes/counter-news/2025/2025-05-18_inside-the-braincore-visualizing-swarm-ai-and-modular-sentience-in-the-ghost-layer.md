## Source and current assessment

Uses dynamic flow and shared attractor patterns to imagine self-directed behavior across a swarm.

Motion in a visualization or a stable output pattern does not demonstrate self-determination or subjective experience. The source supplies a conceptual account rather than measurements.

## Research question

Can the proposed dynamics explain behavior beyond the system’s inputs, memory, and policy?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Define latent-state observations and perturbation tests, then compare causal predictive value against ordinary routing and memory models.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
