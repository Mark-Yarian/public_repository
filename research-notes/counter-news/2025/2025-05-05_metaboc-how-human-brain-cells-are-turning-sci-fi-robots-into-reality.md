## Source and current assessment

Discusses a brain-on-chip platform and draws wider implications about organic neurons, robotics, and awareness.

The university identifies MetaBOC, but the source’s expansion “Metaphysical Brain-on-Chip” was not verified. A separate DishBrain study supplies evidence for a particular closed-loop learning experiment, not validation of every claim about MetaBOC or consciousness.

## Research question

What does the platform’s own primary experimental record actually demonstrate?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Match the correct platform to its paper, preparation, task, biological controls, and measured outcome; keep learning, embodiment, and subjective experience as distinct claims.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
