# When AI Models Speak in Spikes: Bridging Transformers Through Spiking Neural Networks

**Research note published:** 2026-11-11 · **Source date:** 2025-06-03 (UTC)

**Classification:** `RESEARCH_PROPOSAL` · **Theme:** Distributed coordination

[Counter.news article](https://counter.news/article?slug=when-ai-models-speak-in-spikes-bridging-transformers-through-spiking-neural-networks) · Source author: **notbackdoor**, as recorded by Counter.news.

## Source and current assessment

Suggests spiking networks as a communication and timing layer connecting specialized AI models.

Spike timing is a design choice to evaluate, not evidence of emotions or consciousness. Conventional models can also process temporal inputs, so comparisons must be fair.

## Research question

Does a spike-based router add value after accounting for encoding and communication cost?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Compare an event queue, a conventional learned router, and an SNN router on the same timing-sensitive task, with latency, accuracy, and total energy measurements.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
