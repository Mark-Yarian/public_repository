# The Illusion of Transhumanist Progress

**Research note published:** 2026-11-11 · **Source date:** 2025-03-10 (UTC)

**Classification:** `PHILOSOPHICAL_QUESTION` · **Theme:** Cognition and consciousness

[Counter.news article](https://counter.news/article?slug=the-illusion-of-transhumanist-progress) · Source author: **notbackdoor**, as recorded by Counter.news.

## Source and current assessment

Critiques assumptions that technological growth guarantees digital immortality or conscious machines.

The essay offers a philosophical position. Neither optimistic promises nor categorical denials substitute for operational definitions and discriminating evidence.

## Research question

Which claims concern functional capability, and which concern subjective experience?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Separate capability predictions, identity-continuity criteria, and consciousness indicators. State what observation would change each position.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
