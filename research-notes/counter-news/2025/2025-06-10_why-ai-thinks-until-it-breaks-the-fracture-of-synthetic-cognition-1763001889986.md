## Source and current assessment

Uses Apple’s reasoning-model puzzle study to motivate external memory and alternative control structures.

The primary study reports results for specific models and controlled puzzles; it does not prove that all machine reasoning is illusory or that the proposed external architecture solves the failures.

## Research question

Which part of a complexity-related failure changes when external memory or explicit algorithms are added?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Replicate a stated benchmark version with equal budgets and separate parsing, algorithm execution, memory, and search failures. Compare memory and tool baselines before making broad cognitive claims.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
