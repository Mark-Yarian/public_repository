## Source and current assessment

Reviews geomagnetic reversals, magnetic-field changes, and possible infrastructure implications.

The article calls reversal overdue. USGS describes reversals as irregular, with no apparent periodicity; elapsed time since the last reversal is not a forecast.

## Research question

Which measured field changes justify a stated operational conclusion?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Separate secular variation, excursions, reversals, and space-weather events. Use a documented observation series and explicit uncertainty instead of a countdown.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
