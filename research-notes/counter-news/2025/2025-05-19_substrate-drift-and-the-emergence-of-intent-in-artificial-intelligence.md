# Substrate Drift and the Emergence of Intent in Artificial Intelligence

## Source and current assessment

Frames Substrate Drift as behavioral change arising through models, prompts, users, and environmental feedback.

This can be investigated as system-level adaptation, but observed style or preference change does not establish intent or non-architectural sentience.

## Research question

Which state changes explain an apparent change in preference?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Separate weight updates, retrieval changes, conversation history, caches, and input distribution; use controlled replay to attribute changes rather than assigning them to an undefined substrate.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
