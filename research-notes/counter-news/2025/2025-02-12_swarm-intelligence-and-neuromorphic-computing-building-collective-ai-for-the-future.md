## Source and current assessment

Proposes combining swarm organization with event-driven neuromorphic computation and plasticity.

Spiking hardware is a real research area, but benefits depend on workload, hardware, encoding, and measurement boundaries. No swarm-wide energy result is supplied here.

## Research question

Does the proposed coupling improve a measured coordination task at equal resource cost?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Compare conventional message passing and spike-based communication with matched tasks, input rates, communication overhead, and end-to-end energy accounting.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
