# GiaLoop: Glial-Inspired Sleep Cycles for Stable, Self-Pruning AI Models

**Research note published:** 2026-11-11 · **Source date:** 2025-09-25 (UTC)

**Classification:** `RESEARCH_PROPOSAL` · **Theme:** Biological inspiration

[Counter.news article](https://counter.news/article?slug=gialoop-glial-inspired-sleep-cycles-for-stable-self-pruning-ai-models) · Source author: **notbackdoor**, as recorded by Counter.news.

## Source and current assessment

Proposes maintenance and consolidation cycles inspired by glial functions, including pruning, regrowth, routing, and data hygiene.

RigL provides a concrete sparse-training reference, but biological inspiration does not establish that the combined maintenance cycle improves stability or simulates glial biology.

## Research question

Which maintenance operations improve efficiency without harming retention?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Ablate pruning, regrowth, consolidation, and data filtering independently; measure compute, held-out performance, forgetting, and recovery after distribution shifts.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
