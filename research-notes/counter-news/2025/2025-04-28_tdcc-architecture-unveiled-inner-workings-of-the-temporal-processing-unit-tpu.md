## Source and current assessment

Explores reversible instructions, state checkpoints, and coherent execution under a speculative temporal-processing name.

Logical reversibility and restoring a recorded software state must be distinguished from physical backward time travel. The article does not demonstrate the latter.

## Research question

Can reversible execution preserve an explicit computational invariant on ordinary hardware?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Use a bounded software model to test inverse operations, information loss, external I/O, storage cost, and replay consistency; make no claim that replay changes physical history.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
