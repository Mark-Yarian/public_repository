## Source and current assessment

Proposes digital cells with inheritable configuration, controlled variation, resource limits, and population-level governance.

The source is an architectural proposal. Existing digital-evolution research supplies relevant experimental methods, but it does not validate this particular framework or establish sentience.

## Research question

Can a bounded lineage system preserve inherited state and measurable variation across generations?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Compare cloning, checkpoint migration, and controlled mutation under fixed resource budgets; record parentage, divergence, task fitness, and failed descendants.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
