## Source and current assessment

Introduces local interaction, self-organization, and collective behavior as foundations for swarm algorithms.

Useful collective behavior should be measured against a task and baseline. A swarm is not automatically optimal, robust, or conscious.

## Research question

When does local coordination outperform a simpler centralized or independent-agent approach?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Compare convergence, task quality, communication cost, robustness to failed agents, and performance across several environments and random seeds.
