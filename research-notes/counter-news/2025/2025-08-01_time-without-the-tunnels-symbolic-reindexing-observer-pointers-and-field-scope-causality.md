## Source and current assessment

Describes time travel as reassignment of an observer’s symbolic reference within a proposed rendering framework.

Changing an index in a model changes that model’s description or replay state; it does not demonstrate changing an observer’s physical past. The retrieved source ends mid-heading.

## Research question

What observation distinguishes physical time displacement from a state-reconstruction simulation?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Keep a model’s state history and the external measurement clock separate, and define a falsifiable physical prediction before making a time-travel claim.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
