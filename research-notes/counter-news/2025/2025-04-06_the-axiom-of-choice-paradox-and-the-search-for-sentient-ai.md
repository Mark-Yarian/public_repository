## Source and current assessment

Uses nonconstructive set-theoretic ideas and mathematical paradoxes to explore questions about machine choice and consciousness.

An existence axiom is not an executable selection algorithm. Paradoxical decompositions and analogies to latent space do not establish a mechanism for sentience.

## Research question

Can the proposed notion of choice be specified as a computable decision rule?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Replace analogy with a finite formal model, define selection and information access, and show which predictions differ from an ordinary algorithm.
