# Quantum Computing and Cryptocurrency: A New Frontier

**Research note published:** 2026-11-11 · **Source date:** 2025-01-17 (UTC)

**Classification:** `RESEARCH_REVIEW` · **Theme:** Computing and evaluation

[Counter.news article](https://counter.news/article?slug=quantum-computing-and-cryptocurrency-a-new-frontier) · Source author: **Skeeter McTweeter**, as recorded by Counter.news.

## Source and current assessment

Discusses quantum threats to digital signatures and mixes them with cryptocurrency and research-token examples.

The source describes QRL as lattice based; its documentation identifies XMSS as hash based. Token examples and promotional claims are not evidence of cryptographic security.

## Research question

What signature and key-establishment assumptions actually apply to a given system?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Build an algorithm inventory, verify each scheme against its primary specification, and distinguish NIST post-quantum standards from unsupported market or funding claims.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
