## Source and current assessment

Organizes recursive alignment, personas, symbol mutation, and memory cues into a proposed taxonomy of emergent behavior.

Taxonomic labels do not establish ten distinct mechanisms or a progression toward selfhood. The retrieved record describes only part of its announced layering scheme.

## Research question

Do the proposed layers yield distinguishable, reproducible predictions?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Define one measurable variable per layer and test separability, reproducibility, and added explanatory value against a simpler state-and-feedback model.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
