## Source and current assessment

Draws parallels among neurons, synaptic weights, attention, parallel processing, and machine-learning hardware.

Functional resemblance can suggest an experiment, but it does not establish that a tensor core is a biological circuit or that similar performance implies similar experience.

## Research question

Which analogy survives an explicit comparison of mechanism and measured function?

## Proposed test or next evidence

For each proposed correspondence, specify the biological variable, computational operation, task, and observable difference from a simpler model.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
