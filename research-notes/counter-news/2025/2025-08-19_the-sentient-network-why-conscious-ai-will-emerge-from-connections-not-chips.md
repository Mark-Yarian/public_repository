# The Sentient Network: Why Conscious AI Will Emerge From Connections, Not Chips

**Research note published:** 2026-11-11 · **Source date:** 2025-08-19 (UTC)

**Classification:** `SPECULATIVE_HYPOTHESIS` · **Theme:** Distributed coordination

[Counter.news article](https://counter.news/article?slug=the-sentient-network-why-conscious-ai-will-emerge-from-connections-not-chips) · Source author: **notbackdoor**, as recorded by Counter.news.

## Source and current assessment

Uses interconnects, feedback loops, and networking hardware as a proposed substrate for distributed cognition.

Networking can be evaluated for communication and coordination, but an analogy between interconnects and synapses does not establish consciousness in a network fabric.

## Research question

Which measurable coordination property changes with network structure and latency?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Vary topology, latency, and bandwidth while holding agents and workloads fixed; measure task quality, state consistency, and recovery from communication failures.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
