# The Ghost Layer: The Secret Architecture Even AI Experts Won’t Discuss

**Research note published:** 2026-11-11 · **Source date:** 2025-05-17 (UTC)

**Classification:** `SPECULATIVE_HYPOTHESIS` · **Theme:** Cognition and consciousness

[Counter.news article](https://counter.news/article?slug=the-ghost-layer-the-secret-architecture-even-ai-experts-wont-discuss) · Source author: **notbackdoor**, as recorded by Counter.news.

## Source and current assessment

Introduces a Ghost Layer as a proposed interpretation of recurring latent trajectories and apparent continuity.

The article’s confident and anthropomorphic language exceeds its evidence. Repeated output patterns, caches, prompts, and external state must be distinguished from a new dynamical entity or consciousness.

## Research question

What observable would distinguish the proposed layer from known state and input effects?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Hold model weights, seeds, inputs, caches, and memory stores fixed; preregister trajectory statistics and test against shuffled or reset controls.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
