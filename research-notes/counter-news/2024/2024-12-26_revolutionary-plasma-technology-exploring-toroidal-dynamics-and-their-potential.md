## Source and current assessment

Describes toroidal plasma organization and attributes improved energy behavior to a named generator concept.

Toroidal geometry and plasma confinement do not by themselves demonstrate net energy production. The article provides no independent energy balance for the generator claims.

## Research question

Can the claimed improvement survive a complete and independently measured energy accounting?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Define every energy input, output, storage term, and loss before reviewing a claim. Use calibrated uncertainty and independent replication rather than visual plasma appearance.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
