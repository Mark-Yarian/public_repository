## Source and current assessment

Introduces balanced ternary processor components and suggests advantages in representation, arithmetic, and energy use.

Representation compactness does not prove a hardware efficiency advantage. Carry behavior still exists: decimal 2 is represented by balanced-ternary digits (1, -1), since 3 minus 1 equals 2.

## Research question

Under matched hardware assumptions, which workloads benefit from ternary representation?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Compare area, switching activity, error margins, conversion overhead, and energy per completed task against a binary baseline. Report assumptions instead of claiming universal superiority.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
