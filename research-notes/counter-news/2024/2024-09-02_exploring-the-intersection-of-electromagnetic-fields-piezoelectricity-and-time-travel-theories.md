# Exploring the Intersection of Electromagnetic Fields, Piezoelectricity, and Time Travel Theories

**Research note published:** 2026-11-11 · **Source date:** 2024-09-02 (UTC)

**Classification:** `SPECULATIVE_HYPOTHESIS` · **Theme:** Speculative physics and model boundaries

## Source and current assessment

Connects electromagnetic fields, piezoelectric devices, resonance, and gravitational time dilation to proposed time manipulation.

The article supplies no quantitative bridge from an electrical apparatus to usable time displacement. An analogy with gravity is not a measurement or a demonstrated mechanism.

## Research question

What independently measurable effect distinguishes this proposal from ordinary electrical and thermal behavior?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Require a quantitative model and a bounded predicted clock signal first. Compare against electromagnetic interference, thermal drift, vibration, and instrument artifacts; no high-voltage construction follows from this note.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
