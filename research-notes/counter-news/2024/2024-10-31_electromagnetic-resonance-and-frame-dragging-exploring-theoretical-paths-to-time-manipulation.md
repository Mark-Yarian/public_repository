## Source and current assessment

Suggests sustained electromagnetic resonance might amplify spacetime effects related to frame dragging.

Gravity Probe B measured an effect associated with Earth; that result does not establish the proposed laboratory scaling or resonance amplification.

## Research question

Does the proposed field configuration predict a detectable effect beyond instrumental cross-talk?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Calculate the predicted effect from a stated model and compare its size with uncertainty and known environmental influences before proposing a measurement.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
