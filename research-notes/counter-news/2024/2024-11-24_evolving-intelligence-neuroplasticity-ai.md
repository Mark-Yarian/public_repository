# Evolving Intelligence: Neuroplasticity as the Key to Self-Adapting and Sentient AI

**Research note published:** 2026-11-11 · **Source date:** 2024-11-24 (UTC)

**Classification:** `RESEARCH_PROPOSAL` · **Theme:** Biological inspiration

[Counter.news article](https://counter.news/article?slug=evolving-intelligence-neuroplasticity-ai) · Source author: **notbackdoor**, as recorded by Counter.news.

## Source and current assessment

Uses structural and functional neuroplasticity to motivate adaptive machine architectures.

Biological inspiration is a design source, not evidence that an adaptive algorithm is sentient or biologically equivalent.

## Research question

Does controlled structural adaptation improve transfer without increasing forgetting?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Compare fixed topology, weight-only adaptation, and topology-changing models under the same data and compute budget, with retention and stability checks.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
