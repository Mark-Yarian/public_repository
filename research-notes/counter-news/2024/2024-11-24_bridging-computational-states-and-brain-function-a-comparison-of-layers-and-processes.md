## Source and current assessment

Compares binary, ternary, quaternary, and continuous computing states with simplified descriptions of neuronal processing.

These mappings are explanatory analogies. Active dendritic computation and graded signals make a literal mapping from a neuron to a simple digital gate incomplete.

## Research question

Which biological features add measurable predictive value beyond a simplified gate model?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Specify an input-output task, then compare a threshold unit with compartmental or graded models while tracking model complexity and predictive error.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
