## Source and current assessment

Discusses the measured Casimir force alongside the mathematical idea of a rotating Tipler cylinder.

Evidence for the Casimir force does not validate an engineered time machine. The two topics require separate physical models, assumptions, scales, and evidence.

## Research question

Which part of the argument is an established measurement, and which is a proposed spacetime construction?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Create separate evidence ledgers for force metrology and closed-timelike-curve assumptions. Require a numerical feasibility argument before treating their combination as an engineering program.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
