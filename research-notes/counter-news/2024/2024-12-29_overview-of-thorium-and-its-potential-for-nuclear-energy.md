## Source and current assessment

Surveys thorium resources, fuel-cycle possibilities, and arguments for nuclear-energy development.

Thorium-232 is fertile rather than directly fissile; a thorium fuel cycle requires a fissile component. Safety, waste, and proliferation properties must be assessed for a specified reactor and fuel cycle.

## Research question
