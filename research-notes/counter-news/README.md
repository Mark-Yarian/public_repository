## Scope and method

The published Science-category inventory contained 66 records. Metadata screening removed 14 mixed political, policy, career, or marketing items. The available content of the remaining 52 records was retrieved for review; two predominantly humorous or motivational pieces were excluded after content review. The 50 retained notes cover the supplied scientific or technical ideas without copying the articles wholesale. Original authors’ displayed names and source dates are preserved. No Counter.news article, category, publication date, or original text was changed.

Each note separates the article’s focus from the current assessment, states a research question, proposes a test that has not been run, and identifies evidence still needed. The 18-entry reference ledger supports specific background checks and corrections; it does not certify every claim in the source collection. Ten retained records show possible truncation; missing content is not invented.

## Dates, attribution, and reuse

Historical dates come from `published_at` where supplied, otherwise `date_gmt`. Raw timestamps, timezone interpretation, source modification metadata, capture dates and content hashes are in the notes and catalog. These are source-recorded dates; contemporaneous first-publication revisions were not independently recovered. The current app’s published-record content and display precedence were checked; separate public webpage retrieval was unavailable for two sampled article URLs. Source links follow the observed public article route. No blanket external-link availability claim is made.

Current assessments may cite research published after a source article. Those citations belong to the 2026-11-11 assessment, not to a backdated original bibliography. GitHub history records the actual import. Rights in source articles remain with their authors; the notes grant public reading and citation without granting additional rights in the originals.
