# Primary References for the Current Research Assessments

Checked 2026-11-11. These sources support the stated background checks; they do not retroactively become references of the historical Counter.news articles and do not validate the proposed systems. Research reports, experiments, standards, and implementation documentation have different evidential roles.

## GPB

**Gravity Probe B: Final Results of a Space Experiment to Test General Relativity** — Everitt et al., 2011. Primary experiment.

[Primary source](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.106.221101)

Measured geodetic and frame-dragging effects in Earth orbit; does not establish a tabletop time-manipulation device.

## PQC

**NIST approval of three post-quantum cryptography standards** — NIST, 2024. Primary standards authority.

[Primary source](https://csrc.nist.gov/news/2024/postquantum-cryptography-fips-approved)

Distinguishes key establishment and signatures; does not certify cryptocurrency projects or investment claims.

## QRL

**QRL Address Scheme** — QRL project documentation. Primary implementation documentation.

[Primary source](https://docs.theqrl.org/build/address/address-scheme/)

Identifies the XMSS-based hash-signature scheme; used to correct the source article’s lattice-based description.

## DENDRITES

**Dendritic action potentials and computation in human layer 2/3 cortical neurons** — Gidon et al., 2020. Primary experimental paper.

[Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31896716/)

Reports particular active dendritic computations; does not establish that a tree-shaped artificial network is conscious.

## AI-CONSCIOUSNESS

**Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence: Insights from the Science of Consciousness** — Butlin et al., 2023. Research report / preprint.

[Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708)

Supplies an indicator-based assessment framework. Its assessment is tied to the systems and theories considered in that report, not a certification of all subsequent models.

## RIGL

**Rigging the Lottery: Making All Tickets Winners** — Evci et al., ICML 2020. Primary research paper.

[Primary source](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/evci20a.html)

Provides a concrete sparse-training method and reported evaluations; does not validate the combined GiaLoop proposal.

## ILLUSION-THINKING

**The Illusion of Thinking: Understanding the Strengths and Limitations of Reasoning Models via the Lens of Problem Complexity** — Shojaee et al., 2025. Primary research paper.

[Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06941)

Reports results in controlled puzzle environments. Those results do not prove the proposed Counter.news alternative architecture works.

## TELEPORTATION

**Teleporting an unknown quantum state via dual classical and Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen channels** — Bennett et al., 1993. Primary theoretical paper.

[Primary source](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.70.1895)

The protocol requires classical communication as well as shared entanglement; it does not supply the archive’s assumed backward-time communication channel.

## GEOMAGNETISM

**Is it true that Earth's magnetic field occasionally reverses its polarity?** — USGS. Primary scientific agency reference.

[Primary source](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/it-true-earths-magnetic-field-occasionally-reverses-its-polarity?items_per_=&items_per_page=6&page=1)

Describes reversals as irregular with no apparent periodicity, correcting the source article’s overdue framing.

## RADIATION

**Acute Radiation Syndrome: Information for Clinicians** — CDC. Primary clinical authority.

[Primary source](https://www.cdc.gov/radiation-emergencies/hcp/clinical-guidance/ars.html)

A reference for radiation-injury conditions and tissue effects; the archive note does not create a treatment protocol.

## THORIUM

**IAEA TECDOC-2009: thorium fuel-cycle discussion** — IAEA. Primary technical report.

[Primary source](https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/Publications/PDF/TE-2009web.pdf)

Distinguishes fertile thorium from fissile material and discusses fuel-cycle requirements; does not establish blanket safety or waste claims for every reactor.

## ITER

**The ITER tokamak** — ITER Organization. Primary engineering-program documentation.

[Primary source](https://www.iter.org/components)

Describes a magnetic-confinement fusion system; does not validate an unrelated generator’s energy claims.

## METABOC

**Xiaohong Li research profile identifying MetaBOC** — Tianjin University. Primary institutional record.

[Primary source](https://mstu.tju.edu.cn/info/1193/4931.htm)

Identifies the MetaBOC program. It does not substantiate the article’s Metaphysical expansion or its broad consciousness implications.

## DISHBRAIN

**In vitro neurons learn and exhibit sentience when embodied in a simulated game-world** — Kagan et al., 2022. Primary experimental paper.

[Primary source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9747182/)

Reports a specific closed-loop culture experiment. The paper’s operational use of sentience is not a general demonstration of subjective experience or of MetaBOC.

## AVIDA

**Avida: a software platform for research in computational evolutionary biology** — Ofria and Wilke, 2004. Primary research paper / author repository.

[Primary source](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/2h3br-gvn73)

Provides controlled experiments with evolving programs; does not validate the specific Digital Cellular Mitosis architecture or consciousness claims.

## LLVM

**LLVM Language Reference Manual** — LLVM project documentation. Primary technical documentation.

[Primary source](https://www.llvm.org/docs/LangRef.html)

Documents an intermediate representation and its verification requirements; does not make compilation biologically equivalent to metabolism.

## LOIHI

**Loihi: A Neuromorphic Manycore Processor with On-Chip Learning** — Davies et al., 2018. Primary hardware research paper.

[Primary source](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8259423)

Documents a particular spiking processor and workload results; the scope does not imply universal energy advantages or awareness.

## CASIMIR

**Demonstration of the Casimir Force in the 0.6 to 6 micrometre Range** — Lamoreaux, 1997. Primary experimental paper.

[Primary source](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.78.5)

Reports a force measurement; does not demonstrate an engineered wormhole, usable negative-energy supply, or time machine.
