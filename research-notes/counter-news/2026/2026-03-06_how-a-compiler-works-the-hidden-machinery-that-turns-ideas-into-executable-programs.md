## Source and current assessment

Explains a staged compiler pipeline from source text through structured representations, optimization, and executable output.

The pipeline is an educational model; actual implementations vary. LLVM is a concrete reference for intermediate representation, not proof that compilation is biological metabolism.

## Research question

Can each transformation preserve specified program behavior?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Use a small program and inspect representations and verifier results; compare behavior before and after transformations, including failure cases.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
