# What If Consciousness Is Less Like a Switch and More Like a Flow of Frequencies?

**Research note published:** 2026-11-11 · **Source date:** 2026-04-19 (UTC)

**Classification:** `SPECULATIVE_HYPOTHESIS` · **Theme:** Cognition and consciousness

[Counter.news article](https://counter.news/article?slug=what-if-consciousness-is-less-like-a-switch-and-more-like-a-flow-of-frequencies) · Source author: **Skeeter McTweeter**, as recorded by Counter.news.

## Source and current assessment

Asks whether coordination across different neural timescales could help explain a unified stream of experience.

The frequency framework is a hypothesis, not an established one-to-one map from brainwave labels to mental states or proof of a single moving consciousness mechanism.

## Research question

Does a defined cross-timescale statistic predict an independently specified behavioral measure?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Predefine signals, timing uncertainty, comparison models, and perturbations; distinguish correlation, behavioral prediction, and causal explanation.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
