# The Digital Cell: How AI Becomes a Living Architecture

**Research note published:** 2026-11-11 · **Source date:** 2026-05-08 (UTC)

**Classification:** `RESEARCH_PROPOSAL` · **Theme:** Digital organisms and lineage

[Counter.news article](https://counter.news/article?slug=the-digital-cell-how-ai-becomes-a-living-architecture) · Source author: **Skeeter McTweeter**, as recorded by Counter.news.

## Source and current assessment

Develops a bounded digital-cell architecture with identity, state, memory, sensing, resource constraints, adaptation, and checkpointed lineage.

The article usefully separates organism metaphor from implementable properties. It still does not establish a working prototype, demonstrated persistence, or consciousness.

## Research question

Can the proposed boundary, inheritance, and adaptation properties be demonstrated independently?

## Proposed test or next evidence

Test restart continuity, bounded state transfer, parent-child provenance, resource limits, and behavior under changed inputs, with cloning and nonadaptive baselines.

**Test status:** proposed, not run. This note adds no experimental result or independently verified deployment claim.
