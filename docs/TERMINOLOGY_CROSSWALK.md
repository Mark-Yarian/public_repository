# Digital Mitosis: research terms in context

**Research reading guide · Prepared 2026-11-12 · Proposed public companion to the existing collection**

The [Yarian terminology collection](https://yarian.com/terminology) supplies the names and public descriptions below. This page gives narrower, testable readings for the [Digital Mitosis working paper](../papers/Digital-Mitosis/README.md), the [existing research articles](../research-articles/README.md), and the [lineage lab](../public-experiments/lineage-lab/README.md). The short explanations are editorial interpretations, not verbatim definitions from the site. An entry's site label of **Active** or **Core Concept** is a terminology status; it is not evidence of a deployed implementation.

**Selection boundary:** The terminology source contained 100 public entries at review, including 10 explicitly labeled **Lore Only**. This crosswalk selects 12 research-relevant terms; it does not fold game lore, unrelated physics speculation, or unpublished implementation specifics into scientific evidence. One unrelated source entry had detailed text inconsistent with its own title, so this page uses only manually checked entries and reframes claims at an observable level. Links point to public term pages; no internal notes, contact information, or proprietary runtime details are reproduced.

**Evidence boundary:** A public term description is evidence that the term has been defined on Yarian. Independent support for a computational claim comes from the cited paper and a specified experiment. [Ofria and Wilke's Avida paper](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/2h3br-gvn73) establishes one experimental platform for self-replicating programs; it does not validate this proposed architecture. [How to read claims](READER_GUIDE.md) explains the collection's evidence labels.
