# How to Read the SynapticSteel Public Research Library

**Audience:** curious readers, researchers, engineers, potential collaborators, and people evaluating the strength of a scientific claim.

This is a **curated, public-facing research catalog**, not a live laboratory feed, implementation repository, or proof of product capability.

## Reading evidence labels
- **Research area:** an educational orientation to a topic, **not** an experimental finding.
- **Hypothesis:** a testable proposed explanation; not confirmation.
- **Simulation or demonstration:** an illustrative or model-based result; it may not establish real-world performance.
- **Experimental finding:** a stated method, measurement, comparison, and limitations. Read the work itself for its evidence.
- **`INDEX_ONLY`:** the index exists, but no scientific work has been released through this repository.
- **`PUBLISHED_WORKING_PAPER`:** an author-released manuscript; independent peer review is not implied.
- **`PEER_REVIEWED`:** a status that should be supported by independently verifiable publication evidence.
- **`CORRECTED` / `RETRACTED`:** the publication record has changed and should preserve a transparent history.

## How to assess a paper
1. Identify the scientific question and the exact publication version.
2. Check whether the author labels each statement as established knowledge, hypothesis, simulation, observation, or inference.
3. Look for methods, source citations, comparison baselines, missing evidence, and failure cases.
4. Read the limitations and any corrections, not just the abstract or headline.
5. Cite the specific publication, author, version, and URL. The repository catalog is **not** itself a scientific paper.

## What will not appear here
Private source code, unpublished invention details, internal experimental data, credentials, operator interfaces, unreleased manuscripts, and proprietary topology are outside the public publication boundary. Public GitHub issues or pull requests are **not private submission channels**.

## How papers enter the catalog
A released scientific paper requires explicit approval of the **exact version and destination** after factual, rights, security, and IP review. Preparatory infrastructure, broad research interests, and a non-scientific repository test do not count as released science. Released manuscripts are recorded in the [authoritative paper index](../papers/INDEX.md), with complete PDFs, versions, evidence classifications and rights statements. An author’s release approval is distinct from independent peer review.
