# RA-010 — Behavioral drift across model, memory, and environment

**Research article · Version 0.1 · Published 2026-11-11 (America/New_York) · Not peer reviewed**

**Type:** Research synthesis and methods proposal. **Review stage:** Initial critical synthesis. **Data status:** No new empirical dataset or result; proposed comparisons are unrun.

## Research question and argument

A change in tone, preference-like responses, tool choices, or latent trajectories is an observation to explain. Several causes may be available: different prompts, retrieval, histories, caches, model revisions, sampling, and environments. A behavioral label is useful only when its measured variables and alternatives remain explicit.

## Evidence and limits

[Butlin et al., 2023](REFERENCES.md#consciousness) organizes theory-based indicators; it does not turn a recurring output style or self-description into an independent measurement of subjective experience. This review treats drift as a reproducible behavioral question and leaves broader interpretation separate.

## Proposed comparison

1. Specify a behavioral score and task population before examining selected conversations.
2. Record model artifacts, prompts, histories, retrieval, memory, cache policy, and sampling controls.
3. Replay fixed-state conditions and then change one recorded component at a time.
4. Include reset, shuffled-history, and surrogate-trajectory controls for apparent persistent patterns.
5. Test whether a proposed latent statistic predicts held-out behavior beyond simpler observable features.

## Data needed to assess the claim

| Record | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Behavioral definition | A measurable response or action distribution |
| Context revision | What information was supplied and accepted |
| State controls | Weights, memory, cache, and settings |
| Perturbation | The one factor intentionally varied |
| Held-out outcome | Reproducibility and added predictive value |

## Interpretation and failure criteria

If required state is missing, the cause may remain indeterminate. Motion in a visualization or familiar language does not resolve that absence. Reject a new-mechanism claim when ordinary recorded state changes explain the same result.

**Vocabulary — Behavioral phenotype: a measured pattern of outputs and actions under stated conditions.**
