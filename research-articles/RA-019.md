# RA-019 — Attributable communication and bounded agency

**Research article · Version 0.1 · Published 2026-11-11 (America/New_York) · Not peer reviewed**

**Type:** Research synthesis and methods proposal. **Review stage:** Initial critical synthesis. **Data status:** No new empirical dataset or result; proposed comparisons are unrun.

## Research question and argument

Useful agent communication should change a defined task outcome beyond what fixed codes or shuffled messages can explain. The resulting action also needs a record of the information consumed and the permission under which it occurred. Persistent identity, coordination, and execution authority answer different questions.

## Evidence and limits

[ZeroMQ project](REFERENCES.md#zeromq) specifies transport patterns and [Ongaro and Ousterhout, 2014](REFERENCES.md#raft) specifies a consistency protocol. Neither supplies task semantics or execution permission. This article does not adopt a historical implementation narrative as current operational proof.

## Proposed comparison

1. Specify message fields, partner knowledge, action choices, and permitted capability scope.
2. Compare learned messages with fixed codes, shuffled messages, and no-message conditions at matched bandwidth.
3. Test unfamiliar partners and environments, including delayed, duplicated, stale, and contradictory messages.
4. Bind each attempted action to consumed-message identity and the applicable capability decision.
5. Count useful completions, incorrect actions, correct refusals, duplicate effects, and communication cost.

## Data needed to assess the claim

| Record | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Message identity | Content, sender, revision, and delivery history |
| Consumed state | What the acting participant actually used |
| Authority record | Scope and admission decision for the operation |
| Action receipt | Attempted effect and independently supported outcome |
| Comparison population | All tasks, partners, failures, and refusals |

## Interpretation and failure criteria

Recurring strings do not establish semantic agreement. A correct route does not authorize an operation. A successful request is not proof of an independent computational organism. Each interpretation requires its own observable criterion.

**Vocabulary — Agency: the ability to select and attempt actions within a stated environment and boundary.**
