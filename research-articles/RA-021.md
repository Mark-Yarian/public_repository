# RA-021 — Operational indicators and consciousness claims

**Research article · Version 0.2 · Expanded 2026-11-12 (America/New_York) · Not peer reviewed**

**Type:** Research synthesis and methods proposal. **Review stage:** Initial critical synthesis. **Data status:** No new empirical dataset or result; proposed comparisons are unrun.

**Editorial provenance:** Version 0.2 teaching additions were prepared with AI assistance from the already released paper, notes and public terminology; the proposed framework and original article remain attributed to Mark Yarian.

## Research question and argument

A consciousness claim needs an explicit framework and a statement of what the available observation can establish. Fluent self-description, architectural complexity, persistent identity, and biological resemblance are observations or design properties. Their interpretation should not be settled by an evocative label.

## Evidence and limits

[Butlin et al., 2023](REFERENCES.md#consciousness) proposes indicators derived from scientific theories. [Kagan et al., 2022](REFERENCES.md#dishbrain) concerns a particular biological preparation and task. Their scopes differ, and neither is a universal certification of later artificial systems or of subjective experience.

## Proposed comparison

1. Separate capability, learning, persistence, self-modeling, and subjective-experience hypotheses.
2. Choose the indicator framework and comparison criteria before examining compelling examples.
3. Control prompt wording, training-like self-descriptions, memory, architecture, and task exposure where possible.
4. Include negative and simpler-system controls and preserve contradictory observations.
5. Report framework dependence and unresolved interpretation separately from measured behavior.

## Interpretation and failure criteria

Uncertainty is not proof of either the presence or absence of experience. An operational indicator can be investigated without claiming access to subjective states. This is a research-method review, not a consciousness finding.

## Boundary for digital organism language

The [Yarian terminology collection](https://yarian.com/terminology) uses biological terms as research and design language. An independently named descendant, a retained memory, and a stable behavioral pattern would each support a particular operational claim if measured. None is a test of subjective experience by itself. Treat the [Digital Mitosis paper](../papers/Digital-Mitosis/README.md) as a proposed inheritance framework and [the lineage lab](../public-experiments/lineage-lab/README.md) as an illustrative state-copying exercise. Record a consciousness hypothesis separately, with its own indicators, rival explanations, and evidence limits.

**Vocabulary — Indicator: an observable property used as evidence within an explicitly stated theory or framework.**
