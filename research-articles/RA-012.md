# RA-012 — Recurrent state and a proposed estimation layer

**Research article · Version 0.1 · Published 2026-11-11 (America/New_York) · Not peer reviewed**

**Type:** Research synthesis and methods proposal. **Review stage:** Initial critical synthesis. **Data status:** No new empirical dataset or result; proposed comparisons are unrun.

## Research question and argument

Combining a recurrent sequence model with a state estimator is a testable architecture proposal when the estimator has a defined state, transition model, observation model, and noise assumptions. Calling the estimator a mutation-guiding nervous system leaves those requirements unresolved.

## Evidence and limits

[Peng et al., 2023](REFERENCES.md#rwkv) presents parallelizable training and recurrent inference for a specific architecture. [R. E. Kalman, 1960](REFERENCES.md#kalman) develops a linear filtering and prediction framework. A Kalman estimator is not, by itself, a general mutation operator or evidence of awareness. The combined architecture requires its own evaluation.

## Proposed comparison

1. Name the model revision and define the estimated state, units, observation interval, and uncertainty assumptions.
2. Compare the recurrent model alone, an estimator alone, a simple smoothing baseline, and the combined design.
3. Inject known changes, biased observations, missing data, and reset boundaries.
4. Score prediction error, uncertainty calibration, retention, state-reset behavior, and runtime cost.
5. Keep any adaptation or mutation policy separately versioned from the estimator update.

## Data needed to assess the claim

| Record | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Model revision and state | Architecture, retained state, and reset rule |
| Estimator model | Transition, observation, and noise assumptions |
| Ground-truth condition | Known state or independently supported reference |
| Update timing | Observation support and estimator cadence |
| Evaluation | Error, calibration, forgetting, and cost |

## Interpretation and failure criteria

A finite recurrent state does not imply unlimited exact recall. Filtering can improve an estimate under stated assumptions while producing misleading confidence when those assumptions fail. No combined RWKV/estimator benchmark is reported here.

**Vocabulary — State estimation: inferring a defined hidden state from observations and an explicit model.**
