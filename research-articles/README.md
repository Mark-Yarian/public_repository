## Review coverage

This first pass screened **185 published source records** in the requested scientific/technical categories, captured **143 candidate bodies**, and retained **138 research candidates** after initial content screening. Forty-seven records were excluded as fiction, political/news commentary, promotion, personal reflection, or other non-research material. Fifty previously reviewed captures were unchanged and could be reused. The 30 articles synthesize questions and methods associated with **138 retained candidates**; **0 candidates** have not yet been developed into a topic article in this edition.

Capture and initial screening are distinct from a complete claim-by-claim review. This is the beginning of the broader review, not a declaration that every source claim has been verified. Historical implementation narratives are not treated as current operational evidence. The collection uses newly written synthesis and direct primary references. Detailed editorial lineage is maintained separately from the public presentation.

## Reading and reuse

Each article states its research question, evidence limits, proposed comparisons, required data, interpretation criteria, vocabulary, and related reading. Paper links express topic or method relevance, not proof of ancestry or empirical support. RA identifiers belong to this article series and do not fill gaps in the STP manuscript series.

This edition adds research articles and navigation. The 11 manuscript PDFs, existing paper versions, DOI records, publication dates, and 10 companion-note sets retain their released content. No source dates or Git history were backdated.
