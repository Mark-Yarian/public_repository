# RA-023 — Measured physics and proposed device feasibility

**Research article · Version 0.1 · Published 2026-11-11 (America/New_York) · Not peer reviewed**

**Type:** Critical scientific background review. **Review stage:** Initial critical synthesis. **Data status:** No new empirical dataset or result; proposed comparisons are unrun.

## Research question and argument

Combining names of established physical effects does not demonstrate a device that uses their combination. A feasibility review must identify the exact mechanism, relevant scale, units, predicted signal, measurement uncertainty, and missing prerequisites. This article is a standalone critical review because physical time-manipulation claims do not directly support the released industrial-sensing papers.

## Evidence and limits

[Everitt et al., 2011](REFERENCES.md#gravity) reports geodetic and frame-dragging measurements in Earth orbit. [Lamoreaux, 1997](REFERENCES.md#casimir) reports a force measurement. [Bennett et al., 1993](REFERENCES.md#teleportation) requires shared entanglement and classical communication. These results do not establish a tabletop time machine, a backward communication channel, or an unrelated energy generator.

## Proposed comparison

1. Separate established measurements, mathematical constructions, proposed scaling assumptions, and unsupported device components.
2. Require a quantitative prediction and specified units before proposing an experimental apparatus.
3. Compare the predicted signal with thermal drift, electromagnetic interference, vibration, and instrument uncertainty.
4. For an energy claim, define all input, output, storage, and loss terms over the same measurement interval.
5. Replace calendar forecasts with explicit evidence prerequisites and independent replication criteria.

## Data needed to assess the claim

| Record | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Claim and mechanism | The effect the proposed device is meant to produce |
| Model and units | A quantitative relation with stated assumptions |
| Evidence scope | Which primary measurement supports which part |
| Uncertainty budget | Signal size versus plausible artifacts |
| Missing prerequisites | What remains unmeasured or physically unsupported |

## Interpretation and failure criteria

This review rejects the inference from a genuine physical effect to an unvalidated device. It supplies no construction instructions and reports no new physics experiment. Software replay, symbolic reindexing, and physical event time require separate definitions.

**Vocabulary — Feasibility analysis: checking whether a stated design can satisfy quantitative physical and measurement requirements.**
