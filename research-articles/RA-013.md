# RA-013 — Plasticity, maintenance, and retained capability

**Research article · Version 0.1 · Published 2026-11-11 (America/New_York) · Not peer reviewed**

**Type:** Research synthesis and methods proposal. **Review stage:** Initial critical synthesis. **Data status:** No new empirical dataset or result; proposed comparisons are unrun.

## Research question and argument

Pruning, quantization, regrowth, consolidation, retrieval changes, and data filtering are distinct interventions. Combining them into one maintenance cycle makes attribution difficult unless each component can be tested independently. A biological name does not specify which operation changed a measured outcome.

## Evidence and limits

[Evci et al., 2020](REFERENCES.md#rigl) is a concrete sparse-training method that changes connectivity. [Dettmers et al., 2022](REFERENCES.md#quantization) studies lower-precision matrix multiplication. Quantization does not inherently mean removing low-weight parameters, and neither study validates every proposed sleep-like or glial-inspired maintenance cycle.

## Proposed comparison

1. Choose a fixed task sequence and retain an earlier-task evaluation set.
2. Compare the unchanged model with isolated pruning, precision change, regrowth, consolidation, and data-filtering conditions.
3. Evaluate combinations only after recording each component effect under matched resources.
4. Introduce a documented distribution shift and measure new-task learning alongside earlier-task retention.
5. Account for update downtime, temporary memory, calibration changes, and maintenance compute.

## Data needed to assess the claim

| Record | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Intervention identity | What changed and when |
| Earlier-task retention | Capability preserved or lost |
| New-task behavior | Adaptation under the defined shift |
| Resource accounting | Training, maintenance, storage, and inference |
| Uncertainty behavior | Calibration and unsupported-confidence changes |

## Interpretation and failure criteria

Reject a stability claim based only on lower training error or a smaller file. A useful maintenance cycle must retain the capabilities named in its objective within its stated budget. No biological equivalence or combined-cycle advantage is established here.

**Vocabulary — Ablation: removing or isolating one component to test what it contributes.**
