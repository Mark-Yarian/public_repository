# RA-003 — Actuator observations, torque, timing, and health labels

**Research article · Version 0.1 · Published 2026-11-11 (America/New_York) · Not peer reviewed**

**Type:** Research synthesis and methods proposal. **Review stage:** Initial critical synthesis. **Data status:** No new empirical dataset or result; proposed comparisons are unrun.

## Research question and argument

An actuator study needs a mechanical load definition, an electrical measurement boundary, and an independent outcome label. A force expressed in pounds is insufficient to specify a rotary load without the moment arm and operating conditions. Repeated position commands or stable current alone cannot establish equipment health.

## Evidence and limits

[Linux kernel project](REFERENCES.md#i2c) documents Linux userspace access and adapter capabilities, including limitations of basic transactions. A successful bus transaction establishes communication with the addressed interface; it does not establish correct motion, calibration, health, or a working firmware-update mechanism.

## Proposed comparison

1. Define a bounded operating envelope using the actual device rating and test fixture; specify torque, motion, duty cycle, and temperature.
2. Observe current, supply voltage, commanded position, measured motion, and temperature using independently described instruments.
3. Change one permitted load or observation condition at a time and include repeated baseline intervals.
4. Record bus errors, scheduling delays, calibration uncertainty, and acquisition support with the measurements.
5. Use independent motion or source references before attributing a shared structural signal to a particular actuator.

## Data needed to assess the claim

| Record | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Mechanical condition | Torque, moment arm, motion profile, and permitted envelope |
| Instrument identity | Calibration, mounting, and measurement uncertainty |
| Timing support | Acquisition interval, bus transaction, and clock domain |
| Independent outcome | Observed movement and separately supported health label |
| Missing observations | Coverage gaps and bus errors |

## Interpretation and failure criteria

This protocol does not instruct an overload experiment or authorize firmware changes. A correlated rise in current and temperature can motivate investigation; causal attribution needs controlled conditions and adequate measurement coverage.

**Vocabulary — Torque: rotational loading, determined by force together with its moment arm.**
