# Primary References and Evidence Scope

Reference checks support the bounded statements identified in the articles. They are not independent reproductions, complete systematic literature reviews, or retrospective verification of every historical assertion. Original experiments, theory papers, preprints, and implementation documentation play different evidential roles. Versioned preprints are linked to specific revisions where recorded; rolling documentation is identified separately.

## Access limits

Some publisher pages restrict full text or retriever access. A citation can remain valid when unrestricted full text is unavailable; the checked scope above states what was accessed. In particular, the Kalman publisher record was located, but its full publisher text was not accessed and a historical university PDF link did not resolve. The article uses a bounded definition supported by the original indexed abstract rather than claiming a full-paper replication. Publisher errata should be consulted before quantitative reuse of a study.
