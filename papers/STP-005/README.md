## Abstract

Multisensor condition-monitoring systems often reward agreement among channels, but agreement is not equivalent to sensor health or physical truth. STP-005 proposes a layered Sensor Trust contract that separates intrinsic measurement quality, temporal validity, calibration state, channel history, physical plausibility, cross-sensor support, independence/diversity, and model-specific usefulness. Trust is represented as reason-bearing evidence and converted into claim-specific admission rather than a universal sensor reputation.

## Evidence and limitations

Conceptual sensor-trust architecture and proposed tests; no empirical performance claim. The manuscript distinguishes its proposals, analytical examples, and future evaluation from established results. Full references are in the PDF.
