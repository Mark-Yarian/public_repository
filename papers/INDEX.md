## How entries are added
A published paper receives a stable identifier, an abstract, full author attribution, public citation metadata, a versioned document, a source bibliography, a clear evidence-status label, and a release date.

Publication identifiers are allocated against the existing series register before release. Proposed titles, unpublished identifiers, or unreviewed experimental conclusions do not appear here.
