# Public Research Areas

This page lists broad areas of scholarly interest, not undisclosed projects or technical claims.

- **Artificial intelligence:** evaluation, model limitations, reliability, and scientific rigor.
- **Computational systems:** distributed computing concepts and operational evidence.
- **Experimental methods:** reproducibility, attribution, controls, uncertainty, and research continuity.
- **Human-machine research:** interaction methods, transparent decision support, and responsible scientific communication.

Future areas are added only when their public descriptions have passed the same disclosure review as full papers. This page must not be treated as a roadmap, engineering manifest, or claim of demonstrated capabilities.
