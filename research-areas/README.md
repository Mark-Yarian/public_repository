# Public Research Areas

**A navigable introduction to four broad fields of scientific inquiry.**

These areas are explanatory and educational, not a roadmap, internal research inventory, claim of demonstrated capabilities, or indication that a particular proprietary method has been released.

The four public areas below retain the stable Markdown format consumed by the Yarian Research page. The additional reading guides expand the subjects without changing that public API contract.

- **Artificial intelligence:** evaluation, model limitations, reliability, and scientific rigor.
- **Computational systems:** distributed computing concepts and operational evidence.
- **Experimental methods:** reproducibility, attribution, controls, uncertainty, and research continuity.
- **Human-machine research:** interaction methods, transparent decision support, and responsible scientific communication.

## How to interpret this index

- A research area means **a subject of inquiry**, not a released invention, experimental result, or commercial capability.
- Each topic guide explains widely used terminology and questions without publishing internal methods.
- The [paper index](../papers/INDEX.md), not this page, is the authority for exact released scientific works.
- New areas or expanded public descriptions still require disclosure review before publication.
